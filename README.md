## What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| ⬇️ **Download** | Paste a link from almost any site: every quality appears, best first. Instagram, TikTok and X posts and profiles show their videos, photos and music in boxes (stories and highlights too). Pause, resume and queue as many downloads as you like. |
| ✂️ **Edit & Split** | Trim with handles, then split into parts that fit WhatsApp, Instagram, X, TikTok, Shorts, Snapchat or Facebook length and size limits. Portrait, square or wide frames, text and emoji on each part, music, watermark. |
| 💬 **Captions** | AI listens, detects the language and writes captions timed to every word. Translate into 70+ languages, edit, style, then burn them in or save .srt / .vtt / .ass. Works offline once the AI model is downloaded. |
| 🔁 **Convert** | Any video to any video, video to audio, audio to audio, or an animated GIF / WebP. One file or a whole batch. |

**Also included:** a browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave (a Download bar on videos, with your own sign-ins), an AI upscaler (Real-ESRGAN), and automatic updates.

## Download and install (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit)

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/AlZaabiXIV/media-factory-tools-releases/releases/latest) and download **`TheMediaFactoryTools-Setup-X.exe`** (under *Assets*).
2. Run it. If Windows says **“Windows protected your PC”**, click **More info → Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed yet).
3. Pick the AI extras you want. The disk space page shows your free space next to what's needed.
4. Click **Install**. The app opens by itself and, from then on, updates itself: when a new version is out it shows what's new with **Update now**.

Needs about 1 GB of free space (more for the optional AI extras). An NVIDIA graphics card makes captions faster, but any PC works.

## Good to know

- Everything runs on your PC. Nothing you edit is uploaded anywhere.
- Your files go to **Videos › The Media Factory Tools** (change it in Settings).
- Services that lock their videos (DRM), such as Netflix, Disney+ and OSN+, can't be downloaded.
- Only download videos you own, that are Creative Commons, or that the creator allows.
